# Pasta Primavera

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Prep | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Penne pasta | 280g |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 2 tbsp |  | Plus more for drizzling |
| Garlic | 4 cloves | sliced |
| Yellow squash | 1 | sliced into thin half-moons | Use just squash or courgette if you can't find both |
| Courgette | 1 | sliced into thin half-moons |
| Asparagus | 1 bunch | chopped into 2.5cm pieces |
| Carrots | 2 | sliced jullien | this is a Chaos optional addition 👀, (I actually didnt have the squash and all the squashes in lidl where tiny) |
| Cherry tomatoes | 150g | halved |
| Red onion | 3 | thinly sliced |
| Sea salt | 1 tsp |
| Frozen peas | 100g | thawed |
| Pecorino cheese | 75g | grated | Parmesan works well too |
| Fresh lemon juice | 3 tbsp |
| Red pepper flakes | Pinch |
| Fresh basil | 25g |  | Plus more for garnish |
| Fresh tarragon | 10g |  | Optional |
| Black pepper |

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil. Cook the pasta according to the pack instructions until al dente. Drain and toss with a drizzle of olive oil to stop it sticking.
2. Heat the oil in a large, deep frying pan over a medium heat. Add the garlic, squash, courgette, asparagus, tomatoes, onion, salt and several grinds of pepper and sauté for 3-4 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender.
3. Add the pasta, peas, cheese, lemon juice and a pinch of red pepper flakes and toss to combine. Stir in the basil and tarragon, if using.
4. Season to taste, garnish with more basil and serve.

## Notes

- Serves 4
- Prepare 10 minutes; cook 20 minutes.
- Tarragon is optional but highly recommended.
- Chaos has done several mods to this recpie to adjust it to her liking 🥹.
