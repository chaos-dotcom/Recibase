# Red Pepper & Almond Linguine

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Prep | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh medium tomatoes | 400g | roughly chopped |
| Toasted flaked almonds | 20g |
| Basil | 1/2 x 25g pack | leaves only |
| De Cecco Linguine | 150g |
| Sherry vinegar | 2 tsp |
| Olive oil | 4 tsp |
| Manchego | 25g | finely grated |
| Red peppers | 2 | deseeded and cut into small chunks |
| Garlic | 2 cloves | chopped |
| Cooks' Ingredients Crushed Red Chillies | 1/2 tsp |

## Method

1. Bring a saucepan of salted water to the boil and cook the pasta for 10-12 minutes until tender. Meanwhile, heat 2 tsp oil in a frying pan and fry the peppers, turning frequently, for 12 minutes until softened and lightly browned.
2. Tip half of the peppers onto a plate. Leave the rest in the frying pan and add the garlic, chillies, half of the tomatoes, half of the almonds and a pinch of salt. Cook for a further 4-5 minutes until the mixture is softened. Reserve a few small basil leaves and tear the rest into pieces. Add to the sauce with the vinegar and remaining 2 tsp olive oil. Blend until smooth.
3. Thoroughly drain the pasta and return to the pan with the sauce and the reserved pepper and tomatoes. Heat for 1-2 minutes. Serve scattered with the manchego, almonds and reserved basil leaves.

## Notes

- Serves 2
- Prepare 15 minutes; cook 20 minutes.
- Good Health: low in sat fat / source of protein.
- Cook's tip: keep basil fresh for longer by treating it like a bunch of flowers. For best results, trim the ends of the stalks and keep in a glass of water at room temperature.
- Per serving: 2178kJ/519kcals, 17.8g fat (4.8g saturated), 67.1g carbs (13.2g sugars), 9.9g fibre, 17.5g protein, 0.9g salt - 2 of your 5 a day.
