# Coconut & Potato Curry

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Prep | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential Vegetable Oil | 1 tbsp |
| Onion | 1 | finely sliced |
| Garlic | 2 cloves | finely chopped |
| Cooks' Ingredients Garam Masala | 1 tsp |
| Nigella Seeds | 1 1/2 tsp |  | Optional |
| Fine Salt | 1/2 tsp |
| Ground Turmeric | 1/2 tsp |
| Waitrose British Maris Piper Potatoes | 700g | peeled and cut into 2-3cm chunks |
| Essential Garden Peas | 200g |
| Essential Coconut Cream | 160ml can |
| Coriander Leaves |  | sliced |
| Green Chilli and Lime Wedges |  |  | To serve (optional) |

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan over a medium-high heat. Add the onion and garlic and fry for 2-3 minutes until starting to soften.
2. Meanwhile, rinse the cut potatoes well, shuffling them in a bowl of cold water to release as much starch as possible; set aside.
3. Add the spices and salt to the onions and fry for 1 minute more, then add the potatoes, turning to coat in the spices.
4. Add 250ml cold water, bring to the boil, then lower to a brisk simmer for 15 minutes, stirring regularly to make sure the potatoes cook evenly.
5. Stir in the peas and coconut cream, then simmer for 3-4 minutes more.
6. Serve with the coriander, green chilli and lime wedges, if using.

## Notes

- Serves 4
- Some flatbreads or steamed rice would go nicely, too.
- Leftovers: hold on to those Maris Piper peelings! They're full of fibre and delicious roasted with oil, paprika and a pinch of salt.
