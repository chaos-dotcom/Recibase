# Med-style Gnocchi

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Prep | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No.1 Red Choice Tomatoes | 300g | quartered |
| Gnocchi | 500g |
| Garlic | 2 cloves | crushed |
| Nonpareille Capers | 1 tbsp | drained and rinsed |
| Essential Olive Oil | 2 1/2 tbsp |
| Tenderstem Broccoli | 200g | trimmed and cut into 3cm pieces |
| Essential Mozzarella | 150g ball | roughly torn |
| Basil Leaves | Handful | roughly torn | To serve |

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (390°F, gas mark 6). In a large roasting tin (about 21cm x 27cm), gently toss together the tomatoes, gnocchi, garlic, capers and 2 tbsp olive oil. Spread out evenly, season and roast for 12 minutes.
2. Toss the broccoli with the remaining 1/2 tbsp oil and season. Add to the gnocchi tin and scatter with the torn mozzarella. Roast for a final 10-12 minutes.
3. Give everything a light stir and scatter with the basil to serve.

## Notes

- Serves 4
- Basil: this classic Mediterranean herb goes with anything tomato-based - add it generously to most pizza and pasta dishes. It's also great blended with olive oil as an instant dressing for grilled chicken and bean salads.
