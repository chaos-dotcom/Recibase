# Prosciutto, Cabbage & Bean Stew

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity | Prep | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Olive Oil | 1 1/2 tbsp |
| Principe Italian Prosciutto Crudo | 70g |
| Onion | 1 | finely chopped |
| Celery | 1 stalk | finely chopped |
| Garlic | 1 clove | finely chopped |
| Essential Savoy Cabbage | 1/2 | shredded |
| Thyme | 4 sprigs |  | optional |
| Essential Cannellini Beans | 400g can | drained and rinsed |
| White Wine | 100ml |  | optional |
| Cooks' Ingredients Chicken Stock | 200ml |
| Essential Single Cream | 2 tbsp |

## Method

1. Heat 1 tbsp oil over a medium-high heat in a large saute pan or shallow casserole.
2. Add 4 slices of prosciutto and cook for 1-2 minutes on each side until crisp; set aside.
3. Add the remaining 1/2 tbsp oil to the pan and fry the onion, celery and garlic for 8-10 minutes, stirring regularly, until softened.
4. Chop the remaining prosciutto, add to the pan and fry for 1 minute.
5. Add the cabbage and thyme (if using) and cook, stirring, for another 3-4 minutes.
6. Stir in the beans, then add the wine, if using. Simmer briskly until almost all the liquid has evaporated.
7. Add the chicken stock and simmer for 2-3 minutes more, then stir in the single cream and take off the heat.
8. Season (remember the ham is salty) and serve topped with the crispy prosciutto. Remove any thyme sprigs before eating.

## Notes

- Savoy cabbage is particularly effective at soaking up the flavour in stews and stir fries. You can also shred it finely, then cook with lardons for a simple side dish, or stir it into mashed potato with salad onions to make colcannon.
