# Creamy Leek Croustade

## Ingredients

### Base

| Ingredient | Quantity | Prep | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh wholemeal breadcrumbs | 175g |
| Butter or margarine | 50g |
| Cheddar cheese | 100g | grated |
| Mixed nuts | 100g | chopped |
| Mixed herbs | 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml) |
| Garlic clove | 1 | crushed |

### Sauce

| Ingredient | Quantity | Prep | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Medium-sized leeks | 3 |
| Tomatoes | 4 |
| Butter or margarine | 50g |
| Wholemeal flour | 25g |
| Milk | 284ml |
| Salt & pepper | to taste |
| Fresh wholemeal breadcrumbs | 4 tbsp (60 ml) |

## Method

1. Base: Put the breadcrumbs in a basin, rub in the butter, then add the remaining ingredients.
2. Press the mixture into an 11 x 7" (28 x 18 cm) tin.
3. Bake in the oven at 220°C (425°F/Mark 7) for 15-20 minutes, until golden brown.
4. Sauce: Meanwhile, slice the leeks and chop the tomatoes.
5. Melt the butter in a saucepan.
6. Sauté the leeks for 5 minutes, then stir in the flour.
7. Add the milk, stirring constantly, then bring to the boil and reduce the heat to a simmer.
8. Add the remaining ingredients, except the breadcrumbs, and simmer for a few minutes to soften the tomatoes.
9. Check the seasoning.
10. Spoon the vegetable mixture over the base, sprinkle with the breadcrumbs, and heat through in the oven at 180°C (350°F/Mark 4) for 20 minutes.
11. Serve at once.
